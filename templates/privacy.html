Privacy Policy

This policy explains what AK YouTube Downloader collects, why, and how long anything is kept. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Last updated: {{ year }}

1. The short version

There are no accounts, so we hold no names, emails or passwords unless you send them to us yourself through the contact form. Files you download are deleted from the server after they are delivered. We do not sell data, because we do not gather data worth selling.

2. Information we process

2.1 Links you paste

A YouTube URL you submit is used to fetch that video's public details and to prepare your file. It is held in memory while the job runs. We do not keep a permanent record linking a URL to a visitor.

2.2 Server logs

Like any web server, ours may record the IP address, browser user agent, requested page and timestamp of a request. These logs exist for security, abuse prevention and debugging, and are rotated regularly.

2.3 Contact form

If you write to us, we store the name, email address, subject and message you provide so we can reply. You can ask us to delete that message at any time.

2.4 Cookies

The site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store small preferences locally (for example the last format you chose); that stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Files you download

Your file is created on the server, sent to your browser, and then removed. We do not build a library of downloaded media, we do not scan the contents of files, and we do not share them with anyone.

4. Third parties

To prepare a download, our server contacts YouTube's public servers. That request comes from our server, not from your browser, so YouTube does not receive your IP address as part of the download. Thumbnails shown in the preview are loaded directly from YouTube's image servers by your browser.

If this site is hosted with a cloud provider or sits behind a CDN, that provider processes traffic on our behalf under its own security and logging practices.

5. Analytics and advertising

If we later add analytics or advertising, this page will be updated before it goes live, and any provider used will be named here. We will not introduce systems that build personal profiles of visitors.

6. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and to object to processing. Since we hold almost nothing, such requests are usually simple to satisfy. Write to {{ contact_email }}.

8. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS in production. No system is perfectly secure, but because we do not store accounts or payment details there is very little for an attacker to obtain.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy go to {{ contact_email }} or through the contact page.
