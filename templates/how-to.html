How to Download YouTube Videos

The same four steps work on every device. Below the basics you will find specific instructions for Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone, plus fixes for the errors people hit most often.

On this page The four steps Windows and Mac Android and iPhone Saving as MP3 Downloading Shorts When a download fails

The four steps

  1. Copy the link. On the YouTube app, tap Share → Copy link. In a browser, copy the address bar. Both youtube.com/watch?v=… and youtu.be/… work.
  2. Paste it into the box on the downloader page and press Get video. You will see the thumbnail, title and channel so you can check it is the right video.
  3. Choose your format. MP4 keeps the picture; MP3 keeps only the sound. Then pick a resolution or a bitrate.
  4. Press Download. A progress bar shows the transfer, then the file saves to your device on its own. If your browser blocks the automatic save, press Save again.

On Windows and Mac

Any modern browser works — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave, Opera. The file lands in your usual downloads folder:

  • Windows: C:\Users\YourName\Downloads
  • macOS: /Users/YourName/Downloads

If Chrome asks "Keep or discard?", choose Keep. That prompt appears for any file type the browser cannot preview, and it is not a warning about the file itself. To change where files go, open your browser settings and look for Downloads → Location.

On Android and iPhone

Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome, Samsung Internet or Firefox.
  2. Paste the link and download as usual.
  3. The file appears in Downloads, and videos usually show up in your Gallery within a minute.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Download the file. Safari shows a blue arrow in the address bar when it finishes.
  3. Tap that arrow, then the file, to open it in the Files app under On My iPhone → Downloads.
  4. To move a video into your camera roll, open it in Files, tap Share, then Save Video.

Older iOS versions handle browser downloads poorly. If nothing happens on tap, update iOS or try the same link on a computer.

Saving audio as MP3

Switch the format dropdown from MP4 video to MP3 audio, then pick a bitrate — 192 kbps suits most music, 128 kbps is plenty for speech. The MP3 page explains the trade-offs and what tags get written into the file.

Downloading Shorts

Shorts work exactly like normal videos. Copy the youtube.com/shorts/… link and paste it in — see the Shorts downloader for details about resolution and watermarks.

When a download fails

What you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
“That doesn't look like a YouTube video link”The text pasted is not a video URLCopy the link again from the Share menu
“This video is private”The uploader restricted itNothing can be done — private videos are never accessible
“Video unavailable”Deleted, or blocked in this regionCheck the video still plays on YouTube
“Too many requests”YouTube is rate-limiting the serverWait a minute or two and try again
Download stops partwayConnection droppedStart it again; nothing is charged or wasted
Stuck on “Preparing file”A large 4K video is being mergedGive it a moment — big files take longer after the transfer finishes
