About AK YouTube Downloader

A small, independent tool built because most YouTube downloaders are covered in fake buttons and pop-ups. This one is not.

What we set out to build

Anyone who has searched for a YouTube downloader knows the pattern: five buttons that say Download, four of which install something you did not want, and a file that turns out to be 360p no matter what you selected. We wanted the opposite — one input, honest quality options, and a file that is exactly what it claims to be.

How it works under the hood

There is no secret sauce, and we are happy to say what the tool is made of:

  • yt-dlp — the open source project that reads what formats a video actually offers and fetches them. It is maintained publicly and updated constantly as YouTube changes.
  • FFmpeg — merges the separate video and audio streams into one MP4, or extracts and encodes audio into MP3, without re-encoding the picture.
  • FastAPI and Python — the small web service that ties the two together and streams progress to your browser.

No YouTube Data API key is involved, which is why there are no quotas to run out of and no cost to pass on to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not host, mirror or republish anyone's videos.
  • We do not require an account, so we hold no personal profile of you.
  • We do not bundle installers, toolbars or "download managers".
  • We do not open pop-ups or redirect you to another site mid-download.

Our position on copyright

The tool is neutral technology — the same one used by researchers archiving public lectures and creators backing up their own uploads. Using it to redistribute someone else's work is not something we support, and it may be unlawful where you live. The disclaimer and terms & conditions spell this out.
