How to copy a Shorts link

  1. In the YouTube app: open the Short, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. In a browser: copy the address, which looks like youtube.com/shorts/XXXXXXXXXXX.
  3. Paste it above and press Get video.

Extra tracking text after the link, like ?si=…, is harmless — you can paste the whole thing.

What you get

  • The original vertical file, usually 1080 × 1920, exactly as uploaded
  • Original audio, including licensed music tracks that play in the Short
  • A plain MP4 that any phone gallery or editor can open

About watermarks

YouTube does not burn a watermark into Shorts the way some other platforms do, so the file you get is clean. If a creator has added their own handle or logo inside the video, that is part of the footage itself and cannot be removed by any downloader.

Want only the sound?

Switch the format dropdown to MP3, or open the YouTube to MP3 converter.
