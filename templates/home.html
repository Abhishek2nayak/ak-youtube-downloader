What you can download

YouTube videos in MP4

Save any public video in 360p, 480p, 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 2160p, depending on what the uploader published. Video and audio are merged into one clean MP4 that plays in any player, on any device.

YouTube to MP3

Pull just the audio at 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps. The thumbnail is embedded as cover art and the title and channel are written into the file's tags, so your music player shows the track properly.

YouTube Shorts

Shorts links download exactly like normal videos — full vertical resolution, original audio, no watermark burned into the frame.

Why people use AK YouTube Downloader

Real quality options

Most sites show a fixed list of resolutions and then hand you a re-encoded 360p file. We read the formats YouTube actually serves for that specific video and offer only those, so 1080p means 1080p.

Nothing to install

It runs in your browser. No extension, no desktop app, no APK, and nothing that asks for permissions it does not need.

No account, no email

You never sign up. We do not ask who you are and we do not build a profile of what you download.

Files are not kept

Your file is prepared, sent to you, and removed from the server. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored.

Works on mobile

Android saves straight to your Downloads folder. On iPhone the file lands in Files or Photos depending on how you save it — the mobile guide covers both.

Open source underneath

The heavy lifting is done by yt-dlp and FFmpeg, two well-known open source projects, so the download logic is public and auditable.

Common questions

Is AK YouTube Downloader free?

Yes. Every format and every resolution is free, with no daily cap and no paid tier.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The site does the work on the server and hands you the finished file through your browser.

What is the highest quality I can get?

Whatever the uploader published — commonly 1080p, and up to 4K on channels that upload at that resolution. Audio goes up to 320 kbps.

Can I download private or paid videos?

No. Only public videos can be processed. Private, members-only, purchased and age-locked videos are refused.
