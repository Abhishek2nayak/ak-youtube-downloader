Choosing a bitrate

BitrateSize per minuteSounds like
128 kbps~1 MBFine for podcasts and speech
192 kbps~1.4 MBGood music quality — the sensible default
256 kbps~1.9 MBVery close to the source
320 kbps~2.4 MBThe maximum MP3 allows

One thing worth knowing: YouTube's own audio is usually delivered around 128–160 kbps in the Opus or AAC format. Converting that to a 320 kbps MP3 does not add detail that was never there — it just avoids losing any more. For most listening, 192 kbps is the sweet spot between quality and file size.

What ends up in the file

  • Cover art — the video thumbnail, embedded so music players show it
  • Title and artist tags — taken from the video title and channel name
  • Full length audio — no trimming, no ads, no intro jingle from us

Good uses for MP3 conversion

Saving a lecture or podcast episode to listen to offline, keeping a copy of music you have released yourself, archiving an interview for reference, or pulling audio from a video you hold the rights to for use in your own project.

Other formats
